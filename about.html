Our Story

Born from a love of quiet luxury

Lotus began with a simple belief — that a salon visit should feel like a retreat, not a routine. From hushed interiors and considered lighting to unrushed appointments, every detail was designed so you can exhale the moment you arrive.

Today, Lotus brings together senior stylists, skin specialists, makeup artists and nail artisans under one roof — united by modern technique, premium products and genuine hospitality.

  • ✓ Senior artists with 5–12+ years of experience
  • ✓ Luxury, dermat-reviewed product lines
  • ✓ Private consultation before every first service

Our Philosophy

Beauty should be personal, effortless & empowering

No trends forced on you. No rushed chairs. We study your features, lifestyle and preferences — then design looks that feel like the best version of you, and teach you to maintain them with ease.
